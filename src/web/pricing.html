Pricing questions

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. One mailbox, ten cleaning jobs a month, no card required and no time limit. It exists so you can see a real preview of your own mailbox before deciding whether the paid plans are worth it.

What counts as a "cleaning job"?

One preview-and-confirm cycle against one mailbox. Running a preview and cancelling still counts, because the work of scanning the mailbox has been done. Scheduled runs on paid plans do not count against the monthly allowance.

What happens if I hit a limit mid-clean?

Nothing is half-deleted. Limits are checked before a job starts, and the per-job message ceiling is applied at preview time — the preview tells you it was truncated and you can run the job again to continue.

Can I downgrade?

Yes, at any time. If you end up over the new limits, nothing is deleted and existing mailboxes keep working — you simply cannot add new ones until you are back within the plan.

Do you offer refunds?

Payments are non-refundable except where required by law, but we make discretionary refunds where something clearly went wrong on our side. Email support@emailcleaner.xyz.
